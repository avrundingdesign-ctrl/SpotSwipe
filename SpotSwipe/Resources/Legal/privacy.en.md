## 2. Principle: as little as possible

Swipez has no user account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your settings, recent searches and saved places stay on your device or in your private iCloud.

For photos, ratings and AI search we run our own server (the “relay server”). It receives the app’s requests and forwards them to Google or Anthropic, so no access keys have to be stored in the app, where they could be extracted. What the server processes is described in sections 5 to 7.

## 3. Location

If you allow location access, the app uses your location to find places near you and to show distances. For this, your search term and search area are sent to Apple Maps (MapKit), as with any map search on an iPhone. Apple processes this under its privacy policy (apple.com/privacy). We do not receive, store or share your location.

Location access is optional: you can enter an address or pick a place on the map at any time. You can withdraw the permission in the iOS Settings app.

## 4. Place search with Apple Maps

Places, addresses, phone numbers and websites come from Apple Maps. Your search term and search area are sent to Apple for this. We do not receive this data.

## 5. Photos, ratings and opening hours (Google Places)

For the cards, the app loads photos, ratings, opening hours and price level from Google Places (Google Ireland Ltd. and Google LLC, USA). The lookup – the place’s name, its coordinates, the city name and your interface language – goes through our relay server, so Google receives that server’s IP address, not yours. Your own location, your name and other identifiers are not transmitted.

The photos are then loaded by your app directly from Google’s image servers, which unavoidably discloses your IP address to Google. Google processes this under its privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy), which may involve a transfer to the United States.

## 6. AI search (optional)

AI search is off until you turn it on with the ✨ symbol or in Settings. The first time, the app explains which data goes where.

With Claude (Anthropic PBC, USA): the app sends your search request and then the names, categories and short addresses of the places found and their city, plus your interface language, through our relay server to Anthropic. Your location, your name and other identifiers are not transmitted. Our server does not store the content of these requests; it only logs the endpoint, the response status and the processing time. Anthropic processes the data under its privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal/privacy), which involves a transfer to the United States.

With Apple Intelligence: processing happens directly on your device, without any transfer to us or third parties.

If the selected provider is unavailable, the app uses the other one; if neither works, it searches without AI. AI-generated texts are marked with ✨ and may contain mistakes.

The legal basis for the transfer to Anthropic is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time by turning AI search off or choosing Apple Intelligence in Settings.

## 7. Device check and usage limit

To prevent abuse of the relay server, the app identifies itself using Apple’s “App Attest”. Your device generates a key that never leaves it; our server stores a random key identifier, a counter and the time of last use.

This identifier is tied to no name and no account, only to this one installation. It is used solely to prevent abuse and to limit daily requests. Daily counters are deleted after three days, the key identifier after 180 days without use.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a working service protected against abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); for photos and ratings it is the performance of our agreement with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

## 8. Library and iCloud

Saved places are stored on your device. If iCloud is active, Apple syncs them through your private iCloud database (CloudKit) with your other devices. We have no access to it. You can clear the library in Settings; this also applies to your other devices.

## 9. Share, call, website, directions

You trigger these actions yourself. When you open a website, its operator receives the usual connection data; when sharing, you decide where the link goes; directions open in Apple Maps.

## 10. No tracking, no sale of data

There is no tracking, no advertising profiling and no sale of data. We share only what a feature actually requires, and only with the services named in sections 3 to 8. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are used under US state privacy laws.

## 11. Transfers to the United States

Anthropic and Google also process data in the United States. Transfers are based on the providers’ contractual guarantees (Standard Contractual Clauses and/or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, where certified). You can avoid the transfer to Anthropic by leaving AI search off or choosing Apple Intelligence.

## 12. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR), the right to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR) and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).

In practice this mainly concerns the key identifier from section 7 – the only data we keep for longer than a single request. Because it is tied to no name, we cannot link it to anyone without your help; write to us and we will explain how to find and delete it. The quickest route needs no one: delete the app, the key becomes invalid and the server entry is removed after 180 days.

## 13. Changes

This privacy policy is updated when features change. The current version is in the app and at zwischenzeit-api.cleanyourstuff.nl/swipez/privacy.
